Frequently Asked Questions

How is my team chosen?

The system assigns you to a team with the fewest current members. If teams are tied for the lowest count, one of those teams is selected at random.

Can I register more than once?

No. Your email address identifies your existing registration. If you submit again, the system recovers the existing team instead of creating a duplicate.

I forgot my team. What should I do?

Use the “Forgot your team?” option above and enter the exact email address you used when registering.

I did not receive my confirmation email.

Your registration can still be complete even if email delivery is temporarily unavailable. Use the team lookup page or speak with an organizer. Do not register again.

Can I change my team?

Team assignments are managed by the event organizers. Please speak with an organizer if you need a correction.
